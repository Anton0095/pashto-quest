# Week 1 — day-by-day linguistic review checklist

**Source:** `content/LEARNING_CURRICULUM.json` on main commit `ef23d11`. Phrase spellings and English senses below are reproduced **as currently authored**, not validated translations. Follow the [reviewer protocol](REVIEWER_PROTOCOL.md), enter a decision for each stable ID in the CSV queues, and capture actual generated sentence examples rather than replacing them with invented idealised examples.

## All seven days — common checks

- [ ] Exactly identify the speaker, listener, relationship, formality and scene before judging a pronoun or tense.
- [ ] Check Pashto orthography, written vs Laghman spoken variant, intelligible romanisation, precise English sense and context.
- [ ] Distinguish citation form from inflected form: check person, number, gender, case, agreement and, where applicable, transitivity and aspect for each **formId**.
- [ ] Review at least three genuinely rendered sentence instances per targeted grammar family and additional boundary cases as described in [sampling](GENERATED_SENTENCE_SAMPLING.md).
- [ ] Confirm that multiple-choice distractors, constructed sentences and accepted English variants do not change the intended meaning or accept incorrect morphology.
- [ ] Record `ACCEPT`, `REVISE`, `REJECT` or `UNCERTAIN` for each reviewed item, with evidence and reviewer/date. No blanket approval by day.

**School rule:** Descriptions with noun–adjective–present equative and masculine/feminine/inherent-plural agreement. No assigned lexical action verbs on Day 1. Other teaching districts use descriptions.

- **Active expression:** `phrase-d1-home-welcome`, ښه راغلاست — “Welcome!”
- **Tutorial prompts:** `tutorial-language`, ته کومه ژبه زده کوې؟ — “What language are you learning?”; `tutorial-guide-name`, زما نوم څه دی؟ — “What is my name?”
- **Bilal expected player reply:** اوبه سړې دي. — “The water is cold.” `bilal-day-1-response`.

Review:
- [ ] Test the orthographic and idiomatic correctness of School “book/new” and the initial Home, Nature and Market descriptions, not only their English glosses.
- [ ] Distinguish masculine singular, feminine singular and inherent feminine-plural agreement; `اوبه` is not an ordinary singular noun.
- [ ] Review the interpersonal suitability and pronoun/formality of the tutorial questions as Muhammad Jan addressing a beginner.
- [ ] Review the greeting, Bilal's actual question and his correct/incorrect/conclusion lines from the fixed-language queue.
- [ ] Check the Day 1 challenge meaning and accepted choices.

## Day 2 — He / she; SOV

**School rule:** Subject–object–finite verb word order and third-person singular present verbs. This is the first day lexical action verbs are taught.

- **Active expression:** `phrase-day-2-opening`, سهار مو پخیر! — “Good morning!”
- **Bilal expected player reply:** دینه کب ویني. — “Deena sees a fish.” `bilal-day-2-response`.

Review:
- [ ] Confirm the finite verb form for named female subjects and the actual sense of “see”, not “look at”.
- [ ] Review School Read/Write and location-specific verbs, their argument structure and whether an object is required.
- [ ] Check that a verb can first be presented in an appropriate finite form without misidentifying that form as its infinitive.
- [ ] Check SOV ordering and accurate English-to-Pashto matching, including names.
- [ ] Review Ms Hameed's greeting and Bilal's authored lines.

## Day 3 — I

**School rule:** First-person singular pronoun `زه` with the corresponding present-tense finite form.

- **Active expression:** `phrase-day-3-opening`, خوشحاله یم چې تاسې وینم. — “It’s nice to see you”.
- **Bilal expected player reply:** زه کب وینم. — “I see a fish.” `bilal-day-3-response`.

Review:
- [ ] Check first-person conjugations against independent verb stems; do not treat all verbs as taking a mechanically appended ending.
- [ ] Check whether Deena's greeting uses an appropriate formality level and whether her chosen form is natural for the relationship.
- [ ] Contrast Day 2 third-person and Day 3 first-person sentences using the same verb.
- [ ] Review reflexive or omitted subjects, if present in actual outputs, and avoid accepting changes that alter who performs the action.
- [ ] Review optional Lake dialogue and generated distractors in context.

## Day 4 — Informal you; clause coordination

**School rule:** Second-person informal singular `ته` with appropriate finite verbs; coordination only of independently licensed clauses.

- **Active expression:** `phrase-day-4-opening`, ستړي مه شئ. — “May you not be tired”.
- **Bilal expected player reply:** ته کب وینې. — “You see a fish.” `bilal-day-4-response`.

Review:
- [ ] Confirm informal-singular verb forms versus second-person polite/plural forms.
- [ ] Check that Muhammad Jan's greeting is idiomatic and its politeness/number is appropriate; do not equate formal address with the lesson's informal `ته`.
- [ ] Confirm conjunction meaning and compound-clause naturalness; individually valid clauses are not automatically natural together.
- [ ] Check Bilal's unusual-fish story and question/reply pragmatics.
- [ ] Compare real challenge sentence-construction choices and accepted alternatives.

## Day 5 — Polite/plural you; first noun plurals

**School rules:** Respectful or plural second-person forms `تاسې` / `تاسو` and new direct noun plurals with gender/number agreement.

- **Active expression:** `phrase-day-5-opening`, السلام علیکم. — “Peace be upon you”.
- **Bilal expected player reply:** تاسې کب وینئ. — “You see a fish. (polite/plural)” `bilal-day-5-response`.
- **Actual form-review example:** `lemma:n-house:licensed:1`, کورونه — recorded “houses” (source form awaiting native review). The current review pack includes the exact `formId`, evidence and prerequisites.
- **Actual screenshot evidence:** [Day 5 plural flashcard](../rpg/dialogue-morphology/day5-plural-form.png) and [Day 5 generated activity](../rpg/dialogue-morphology/day5-plural-sentence.png).

Review:
- [ ] Verify direct plurals *individually*; do not assume a universal suffix, direct/oblique equivalence or that every mass noun has a conventional count plural.
- [ ] Verify gender, adjective and present-equative agreement with at least three different reviewed nouns, and flag missing supporting adjective forms.
- [ ] Distinguish one respected addressee from several addressees. Review `تاسې` / `تاسو` as deliberate variant decisions, not accidental interchangeable answers.
- [ ] Check the greeting-and-response contexts and the Day 5 gathering near the Lake.
- [ ] Ensure Day 5 plural-first introductions are contextually understandable without falsely claiming earlier exposure.

## Day 6 — We; Hills invitation

**School rules:** First-person plural including the speaker, compound subjects and predicates; continued plural-noun practice.

- **Active expression:** `phrase-day-6-opening`, ستاسې په لیدو خوشحاله شوم. — “Nice to meet you”.
- **Bilal expected player reply:** موږ کب وینو. — “We see a fish.” `bilal-day-6-response`.

Review:
- [ ] Verify `موږ` / `مونږ`, first-plural finite verb forms and whether the pronoun includes the current speaker.
- [ ] Review Deena's move from Lake to Hills and Medina's introduction: relational context, speaker/listener identity, age-appropriate register and naturalness.
- [ ] Check joint and compound predicates; verify coordination, argument roles and semantic collocations.
- [ ] Revisit the Week 1 archival uncertainty queue in [LANGUAGE_REVIEW.md](../../LANGUAGE_REVIEW.md#archival-attestation-review-queue): running, walking, carrying/baskets, chairs, helping Muhammad Jan and answers at School.
- [ ] Confirm the player's expected Bilal answer is compatible with the question on that day.

## Day 7 — They; Week 1 exam

**School rules:** Third-person plural `هغوی`, distinguishing a group external to speaker/listener, plural agreement, and cumulative assessment.

- **Active expression:** `phrase-day-7-opening`, وعلیکم السلام. — “And peace be upon you”.
- **Bilal expected player reply:** هغوی کب ویني. — “They see a fish.” `bilal-day-7-response`.

Review:
- [ ] Verify plural third-person meaning, including cases where the written verb surface resembles the third-person singular.
- [ ] Test cumulative `زه`, `ته`, `تاسې`, `موږ`, `هغوی` contrasts with appropriate verb forms.
- [ ] Review greeting response, Edris's Market story and Bilal's conclusion without prematurely revealing future narrative.
- [ ] Audit the **25-question Day 7 exam** for correct Pashto, English targets, alternative accepted forms and distractors.
- [ ] Confirm appropriate plural agreement when nouns and verbs co-occur.

## Sign-off per day

For each day record: `reviewer_code`, exact review date, source commit, reviewed stable IDs, actual sentence sample IDs, accepted vs unresolved items, issues requiring corrections, remaining dialect choices, audio evidence and reviewer's explicit disposition.

A day is **not** native-approved because its UI works, a quiz passes, every CSV row has been opened, or its base lemmas were previously owner-approved. Close the gate only through [Milestone A acceptance](MILESTONE_A_LANGUAGE_GATE.md).

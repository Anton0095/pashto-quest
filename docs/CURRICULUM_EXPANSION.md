## Audio and offline behaviour

New active expressions register in the existing `wordById`/audio manifest system. Their file-safe stable audio IDs are `phrase-d1-home-welcome` and `phrase-day-2-opening`; no separate player or speech engine is introduced. An optional recording is named `audio/<audioContentId>.mp3` and picked up by rebuilding. No recordings are bundled in this checkout.

MP3s take priority. The same browser engine otherwise prefers a detected `ps-AF` neural voice, then other suitable Pashto voices, handles delayed `voiceschanged` events even when unrelated voices loaded first, and retains the explicit warned `ps-AF` request if no Pashto voice is identified. Declared English/Persian/Urdu voices are never selected as Pashto. Preparing/requesting speech is not reported as successful playback; status changes on actual `onplay`/`onstart`, and errors warn honestly. Navigation/Library changes cancel pending and active playback. Visible dialogue has its own accessible status mirror of the shared player.

All text, expressions and curriculum data are embedded by the existing single-file builder. Audio remains optional; unavailable pronunciation cannot block reading, recognition or examination. Automated Chromium and mocked Windows-style voices do **not** verify Windows hardware, a native speaker's pronunciation or iPhone Safari. Those remain untested here.

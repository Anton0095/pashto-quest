# Generation capacity and evidence gaps

Diagnostic capacity under provisional form licenses. Runtime builds no sentence catalogue at startup: it compiles after actual study/grammar unlocks. See INSTRUCTIONAL_DELIVERY_AUDIT.json for measured delivery. An exact match to an archived text is still dynamically composed from forms, never retrieved as a teaching target. Clause-pair capacity is algebraic, not an enumerated list or a native-language approval.

Inventory: 473 lemmas / 607 forms; 181 composition-eligible forms. 29 typed templates across 28 families.

**This is not the delivery percentage.** [The measured delivery report](INSTRUCTIONAL_DELIVERY_REPORT.md) checks actual lessons and assessments. There are zero playable authored sentence fallbacks; the [six fixed expressions](FIXED_PHRASE_EXCEPTIONS.md) are the only whole-phrase exceptions.

## Blocked combinations

- **unrestricted_quantities_1_to_5**: Masculine cardinal One needs an attested cardinal NP (یو ځای is an idiom); numeral Four/Five need direct counted-NP licenses; feminine Two needs its own evidenced form. Three is licensed from fish/baskets only in direct count NPs. Exact IDs: lemma:w2-d9-vocab-1:form:0, lemma:w2-d9-vocab-2:form:0, lemma:w2-d9-vocab-4:form:0, lemma:w2-d9-vocab-5:form:0
- **arbitrary_obliques**: Only stored whole spatial and possessive chunks are licensed. Direct noun homographs do not establish oblique cases.
- **unsupported_tenses**: Past/perfect/future/subjunctive/imperative are outside the Days 1–14 curriculum; no paradigms licensed.

`content/GENERATION_AUDIT.json` lists every unlicensed/partially usable form by stable ID, every missing present-person conjugation cell and nominal plurality/oblique and adjective agreement gaps. Unknown features are never wildcards. A missing feature can exclude a particular role while other evidenced roles remain eligible. No stem, inflection, native review or recording is invented.
